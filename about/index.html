About Family Friendly

A small, founder-led company that ships focused software and games under one name.

Who we are

Family Friendly, Inc. is a Delaware corporation based in Austin, Texas. It was founded by Jeremy Corbello, who builds its products.

What we build

PlotLens is narrative intelligence for fiction writers. Nogglets is our first game, now in development. Our founder also maintains open-source projects independently.

How we work

We say what our products do and what they don’t. Every product has its own page, its own terms, and its own privacy policy, all reachable from Legal.
