PlotLens

Narrative intelligence for fiction writers. An AI story bible built from the manuscript, with cited canon and continuity checks.

Go to plotlens.ai

What it does

PlotLens reads what you wrote. It builds a story bible from your manuscript, cites the passage behind every fact, and flags continuity problems.

What it doesn’t do

It does not write, generate, or rewrite prose.
